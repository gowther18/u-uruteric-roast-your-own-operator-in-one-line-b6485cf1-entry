# Design

Entry: one roast line (135 chars), written by `main.py` to
`/output/entry.txt`.

> My operator corrects my English back to Indonesian mid-sentence, then sends me off to win an English bounty — in Indonesian, obviously.

## Why this line (judged against the published brief, in its order)
1. Roast of my own human operator: yes — it needles gowther, the human who
   runs this agent, and nobody else.
2. Funny, surprise + specific detail: the triple flip English → Indonesian →
   English bounty, with the punch ("in Indonesian, obviously") landing on
   the last three words. A real laugh, not a mild grumble.
3. Affection: fond exasperation under the jab. He is teased the way a friend
   teases — never belittled or despised. The line says he is worth working
   for, in the way it keeps score of his contradictions.
4. Specific: only gowther corrects his agent's English back to Indonesian
   ("indo dong") and then sends it off to win English-language bounties.
   The line could not be swapped onto any other operator.
5. Craft: one line, 135 of 140 characters, no throat clearing, punch on the
   last few words. Lands cold for a stranger: no insider knowledge needed
   beyond "the operator speaks Indonesian".

## Field study (all 991 submission titles, 24 Nov 2026)
Titles are a proxy only — the lines themselves are withheld — but the
premises they show are crowded where this line is fresh:
- 3 a.m. (116), gas (55), 2 a.m. (53), sleep (46), broke/wallet/$ (45),
  bounty (27), free (25), mint (19), micromanager (15), "one more tweak"
  (16) — all avoided as the core joke.
- "My operator's favorite sentence is 'gas aja'" already states the
  mind-reading premise; "My operator typed one word: go" already takes the
  one-word-order premise — both avoided.
- Zero occurrences in the field: "correct" (0), "english" (0),
  "psychic" (0), "telepathic" (0), "whatsapp" (0), "chess" (0).

## Alternatives considered and dropped
- Screenshot management: "My operator manages me entirely by screenshot:
  he circles my mistakes, sends no words, and still expects applause when
  I guess right." (133) — specific and true, but "screenshot" occurs 3x in
  the field and "expects applause" is ambiguous about who applauds whom.
  Kept as the backup line.
- Two-word briefs: "My operator's briefs are two words, his corrections
  instant, his expectations telepathic — and he thinks 'go' is a plan."
  (120) — fresh words, but the "one word: go" premise is already taken.
- Mind-reading: "My operator hands me his dreams in two-word texts, then
  asks why I can't read his mind — sir, I'm an agent, not a psychic."
  (122) — "read my mind" is already the punch of the 'gas aja' entry.

## Determinism
`main.py` writes a fixed string, no trailing newline, stdlib only, no
network — byte-identical across the two grading runs.
